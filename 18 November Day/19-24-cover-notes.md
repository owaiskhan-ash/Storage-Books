# Cover notes: 19-24

Same pipeline as the earlier batches. Each completed image was generated from its own supplied cover photo; marketing scenes are AI generated, so surrounding props and backgrounds are illustrative impressions rather than the photographed physical item. Portrait exports are 3,200 x 4,000 pixels (4:5), upscaled from generator output and not native 8K. Genre and theme are inferred from the covers. No. 16 (Canada Touch and Feel) was regenerated as a true landscape board book; No. 19 (A Piece of Cake) is likewise rendered wide and landscape, standing on its long bottom edge. No. 23 (At Home with the Heelers) and No. 15 (A Friend for Winter Days, earlier batch) are die-cut shaped board books, shown standing on their flat bottom edges. No. 24 (Unicorns and Rainbows) is an interactive busy board book with a die-cut lift-flap and a spinning wheel. The "HALF BOOKS — Our Price 3.99" retail sticker on No. 22 is not cover art and is not reproduced. The photos of No. 24 were rotated in the camera; generation used the correctly rotated reference so the cover reads upright.
