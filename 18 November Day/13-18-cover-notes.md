# Cover notes: 13-18

Same pipeline as the earlier batches. Each completed image was generated from its own supplied cover photo; marketing scenes are AI generated, so surrounding props and backgrounds are illustrative impressions rather than the photographed physical item. Portrait exports are 3,200 x 4,000 pixels (4:5), upscaled from generator output and not native 8K. Genre and theme are inferred from the covers.

The photos of No. 13 (Noah), No. 16 (Canada), No. 17 (Teresa) and No. 18 (In the Wind) were rotated in the camera; the images were generated from correctly rotated references so the covers read upright. No. 15 (A Friend for Winter Days) is a snowman-shaped die-cut board book standing on its flat bottom edge. No. 14 (Farm Noises) is a lift-the-flap board book with colored tab markers on its right edge. No. 16 (Canada, Scholastic First Steps) is a landscape board book but was rendered by the generator as a nearly square portrait book — the cover artwork, flag and layout are kept close to the original, but its true landscape proportions are not exact; a regeneration in landscape format may be requested. The "BUY 2 GET THE 3RD FREE" retail promo sticker on No. 16, the École Campbelltown School barcode sticker on No. 17 and the blank white sticker on No. 18 are not cover art and are not reproduced. Exclusions aside, covers were kept as close to the photographs as the generator allows, and the printed QR badge on No. 11 (previous batch) remains part of that cover.
