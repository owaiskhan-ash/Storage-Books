# 28 November Night Upload

Ten children's books analysed from cover photographs and rendered as hyper-realistic
book marketing images for the 28 November night collection. Each render keeps the
original cover artwork, lettering and layout intact, brightens the cover slightly,
confirms the upright orientation of the source photograph, and stands the book
perfectly upright, dead-on and un-skewed as the hero of a 4:5 frame. Every image is a
single continuous scene: no compositing, no collage, no added text overlays.

Nine of the ten titles are rendered. The one licensed Marvel comic was declined by the
image content filter on its single permitted attempt and is skipped, as requested.

## Skipped: titles that could not be generated

One title was skipped after a single attempt, declined by the image content filter
because of the licensed Marvel character artwork on the cover. No duplicate attempt was
made, as instructed.

1. **Marvel Adventures Spider-Man: Make Mine Mysterio!** — Marvel. Filtered on the first
   attempt; the Spider-Man and Mysterio cover is licensed Marvel character art.

The nine remaining titles are rendered. Every source photograph was already upright, so
no rotation was required; each book is presented standing perfectly vertical, facing
straight forward, with the cover un-skewed and slightly brightened.

Two small notes on the pass: in **05** the floating golden egg sits just in front of the
lower left corner of the book, and in **10** the floating ammonite drifts slightly across
the green title band. The covers remain fully legible and unaltered in both.

Source cover photographs are kept outside the repository under `.source-covers/`, which
is git-ignored, matching the convention used for earlier collections.
